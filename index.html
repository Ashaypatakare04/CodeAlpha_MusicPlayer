ABOUT THE ARCHIVE

Between the Frames · Digital Listening Archive

Between the Frames is an original digital listening archive and editorial music player conceived and developed as part of the CodeAlpha Frontend Development Internship (Task 4 — Music Player). Rejecting conventional commercial streaming layouts characterized by cluttered dashboards, neon aesthetics, and algorithmic noise, this project envisions music through the lens of independent print publications and fine art catalogues.

Engineered exclusively with semantic HTML5, responsive CSS3 with modular design tokens, and clean Vanilla JavaScript powering the native HTML5 Audio API, the application delivers uninterrupted real-time audio playback, interactive scrubbable seeking, fluid volume ramping with memory-state mute, dynamic playlist synchronization, and full keyboard accessibility.

01

Native HTML5 Audio Engine

Engineered around native browser audio events without bloated third-party wrappers, ensuring immediate load times and uncompromised audio fidelity.

02

Editorial Design Tokens

Dual warm palettes in light and dark modes with Cormorant Garamond serif headings, Plus Jakarta Sans controls, and intentional whitespace.

03

Complete Keyboard Navigation

Accessible hotkeys for playback, seeking, track skipping, volume graduation, and mode toggles designed for rapid, seamless interaction.

04

Local Audio Extensibility

Seamless drag-and-drop or file selection to inject your own local MP3s into the catalogue with automated duration reading and metadata extraction.
